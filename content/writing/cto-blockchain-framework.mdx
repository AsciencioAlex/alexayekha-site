## The Question Every Tech Executive Gets

> *"Should we use blockchain for this?"*

After architecting and scaling production platforms across crypto infrastructure, fintech, and enterprise logistics, my answer usually starts with a simple reality check: **Blockchain is an expensive, distributed consensus engine—not a default storage layer.**

Here is the framework I use to evaluate blockchain proposals with engineering teams, boards, and strategic stakeholders.

---

## The Core Decision Matrix

A blockchain architecture is only justified when **all three** of the following criteria are true simultaneously:

1. **Multiple Independent Entities Need Write Access:** Multiple parties must record and update state.
2. **Hard Trust Boundaries Exist:** The participating parties do not—and should not—fully trust a single centralized operator.
3. **Immutability & Verifiable Auditability are Critical:** Historical records must be cryptographically tamper-evident without relying on a central authority.

> **Rule of Thumb:** If any single condition above is false, a centralized, high-throughput relational database (e.g., PostgreSQL) with proper access controls will perform better, cost less, and deploy faster.
